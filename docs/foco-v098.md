# Foco 0.9.8

versionName `0.9.8`, versionCode `23`. Same `signing/foco-debug.keystore`.

## Two states

The pill toggles. **Avisos** is off: outline bell, hairline, the listener does not cancel. **Foco** is on: paper fill and a filled bell, and the listener cancels every notification it can see. TalkBack says `Modo de avisos, Avisos` or `Modo de avisos, Foco`. Without a listener the pill says **Activar avisos**; the next time it connects, Foco turns on. A stored in-scope pause becomes that cancel-all. A chosen off stays off. Pausar Trabajo stays its own control.

## One page title

Personal, Trabajo, and Apps already print their name, so the pager cue keeps the dots and does not repeat the word. Reloj, Comida, and a default Agenda still use the cue as their only title.
