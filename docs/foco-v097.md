# Foco 0.9.7

versionName `0.9.7`, versionCode `22`. Same `signing/foco-debug.keystore`.

## Home opens on Personal

The center Home button, a new HOME intent, and coming back from another app scroll to the first visible Personal page. Reloj stays one swipe away. Cold start still uses that same landing page.

## The pill always says Foco

The three modes stay: off, in-scope cancel, and cancel-all. The word on the pill stays **Foco**. An outline bell is off, a filled bell is the in-scope mode, and a struck bell with a thicker Paper border cancels everything the listener can see. TalkBack still hears Sin pausa, Foco, or Todo en pausa. Without a listener the pill says **Activar avisos**. A change ticks the haptic. Pausar Trabajo stays its own control.
