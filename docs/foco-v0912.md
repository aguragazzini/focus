# Foco 0.9.12

versionName `0.9.12`, versionCode `27`. Same `signing/foco-debug.keystore`.

## Trabajo pill

Pausar Trabajo on the Trabajo page uses the same pill as Avisos / Foco. Height is the touch target, the shape is a full capsule, the label is 16sp medium, and a press scales to 0.98. Off is ink elevated with a 1dp line and Paper dim. On is Paper at 11% with a 1.5dp Paper border and a Paper label. It sits under the page title with the same gap Personal uses.

Off says «Trabajo». On says «Pausado». The tap still only hides or shows the Trabajo apps in Foco. It does not write quiet mode.

The bell stays on the Avisos pill. Personal still says «Avisos» or «Foco». The page title is still printed once: the cue does not repeat Trabajo, and the work search header does not print it again. Ajustes and the overflow still say «Pausar Trabajo» and «Reanudar Trabajo».
